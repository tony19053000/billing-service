Frequently Asked Questions

What happens when a customer upgrades mid-cycle?

Upgrades take effect immediately. Tallyo computes exact second-level proration, crediting the unused time from the previous tier and billing only the net difference on an immediate invoice. The higher credit allocation is granted on the spot.

Can I switch between monthly and annual billing?

Yes. Switching intervals from the customer portal calculates proration automatically. Annual billing includes two months free of charge on all paid tiers.

How does Tallyo prevent double billing on usage requests?

All usage ingestion requests support an Idempotency-Key header. Network retries with the identical key return the original response without executing duplicate operations.

Do unused credits carry over each month?

Plan-granted credits refresh at each billing cycle. Top-up credit packs purchased separately stay in your workspace balance permanently until consumed.
