Our Mission

Engineered for software teams who build to scale

Tallyo was built on a simple conviction: modern billing shouldn't require an engineering team to maintain.

Why We Built Tallyo

Every high-growth software company encounters the same transition: starting with a simple flat-rate subscription, and quickly evolving into hybrid monetization. Customers want seat-based predictability combined with pay-as-you-go consumption for compute, API invocations, and AI tokens.

Legacy billing gateways force engineering teams into an endless maintenance cycle: custom cron jobs, fragile reconciliation spreadsheets, asynchronous webhook races, and manual proration math. We built Tallyo to provide an autonomous, unified billing engine that handles recurring plans, credit drawdowns, and customer portals out of the box.

Deterministic Reliability

No background timers or unpredictable clock drifts. Everything is deterministic, reproducible, and verifiable with simulation clocks.

Financial Precision

Integer-based ledger accounting in USD cents. Every invoice line item, tax calculation, and credit balance reconciles to the penny.

Sub-Millisecond Entitlements

Cached feature permission checks built for core application request lifecycles. Zero external database overhead on your user paths.

Customer Sovereignty

Self-serve customer portals empower your end-users to manage payments, monitor consumption, and download PDF tax receipts directly.

Global Infrastructure

Headquartered in San Francisco with distributed engineering across North America and Europe, Tallyo processes millions of billing transactions and credit metering events for modern software businesses worldwide.
