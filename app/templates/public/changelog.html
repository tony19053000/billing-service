Release Notes

Product Changelog

Continuous improvements, new features, and infrastructure updates shipped to the Tallyo platform.

September 20, 2026

AI Token Metering & Multi-Pack Top-Ups

Added dedicated native support for ai_tokens consumption. Workspaces can now ingest raw LLM token counts and automatically bill 1 credit per 100 tokens with automated ceiling rounding.

Highlights:
  • Zero-latency credit balance validation on inference streaming calls.
  • New top-up pack checkout supporting 5,000 credit blocks at $10.00 each.
  • Real-time telemetry charting on the customer dashboard.
August 14, 2026

Instant Second-Precision Prorated Invoicing

Upgrades and downgrades now calculate remaining billing period seconds down to the exact timestamp. When a customer upgrades from Pro to Business, unused days on Pro are refunded as a line item credit.

Immediate charges are initiated with clear audit lines detailing both the credited prior tier and the newly provisioned tier.

July 28, 2026

Printable Vector PDF Invoices & Tax IDs

Introduced the dedicated printable invoice route at /api/v1/invoices/{id}/pdf. Includes formal company letterhead, tax identification registration numbers, breakdown of subtotal and total, and paid confirmation timestamps.

June 15, 2026

Deterministic Simulation Test Clocks

Replaced asynchronous background workers with an on-demand test clock. Developers can advance workspace time by days or months synchronously, guaranteeing deterministic test suites and instant billing cycle validation.

May 02, 2026

Pre-Computed Workspace Entitlements Cache

Shipped the workspace_entitlements cache layer. Upstream SaaS backends can now verify feature permissions (sso_saml, audit_log, webhooks) with single-digit millisecond response times.

April 11, 2026

Operator Dashboard Telemetry & MRR Analytics

Platform operators now have access to comprehensive business telemetry: Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR), Annual Run Rate (ARR), churn statistics, and plan tier distribution pie breakdowns.

March 04, 2026

High-Throughput Idempotency Engine

Added the Idempotency-Key header specification to ensure that network dropouts and retry cascades do not trigger duplicate ledger deductions.

February 18, 2026

General Availability: Tallyo Core Engine

Official launch of the Tallyo subscription and usage billing platform. Initial release features Starter, Pro, and Business tiers, credit ledger drawdowns, and self-serve customer management.
