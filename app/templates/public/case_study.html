The Challenge: Hybrid Subscription + Compute Token Spikes

Northwind Analytics provides an enterprise business intelligence engine used by Fortune 500 retailers and financial analysts. Customers pay a base monthly subscription to grant dashboard access to analysts, but heavy compute workloads — such as query execution and AI vector lookups — vary drastically week to week.

Prior to Tallyo, Northwind attempted to manage billing through custom Python cron jobs linked to Stripe raw invoices. This approach frequently failed under load:

  • Cron jobs timed out during heavy month-end runs, leading to missed charges and underbilled consumption.
  • Mid-cycle seat upgrades caused proration drift because custom scripts failed to account for exact billing period seconds.
  • Engineering spent days every quarter maintaining custom gating logic for enterprise features like SAML SSO and audit logs.
“We are in the data business, not the billing infrastructure business. Our engineers were spending 20% of their sprints writing idempotency wrappers and prorated credit notes. Tallyo solved this completely out of the box.”
— Maya Thorne, VP of Engineering, Northwind Analytics

The Implementation: High-Precision Prepaid Drawdown

Northwind integrated Tallyo using our REST API in under a week. They modeled their billing architecture around three core components:

1. Instant Entitlement Verification

The Northwind core web app checks customer feature gates against Tallyo's cached entitlement endpoint: 

GET /api/v1/workspace/features/sso_saml/check
Response: { "allowed": true }

2. High-Frequency Token Metering with Idempotency

Every batch query dispatches a usage event with an SHA-256 idempotency key to prevent accidental duplicate charges during network timeouts: 

POST /api/v1/usage
Headers: Idempotency-Key: query_job_892348_batch
Body: { "metric": "ai_tokens", "quantity": 12500 }
Cost: 125 credits deducted immediately from prepaid balance

3. Automated Self-Serve Customer Portal

Instead of building custom billing dashboards, Northwind directs their customers to Tallyo's white-labeled portal where users can download PDF tax invoices, review credit usage history, and add payment methods.

The Results

By migrating to Tallyo, Northwind eliminated billing reconciliation delays entirely. Today, when Northwind's finance team prepares monthly revenue recognition, Tallyo's immutable credit ledger and paid charges reconcile to the penny.

Furthermore, self-serve plan upgrades have increased expansion revenue by 34%, as clients seamlessly upgrade from Pro to Business to unlock SAML SSO and unlimited seats without contacting sales.
